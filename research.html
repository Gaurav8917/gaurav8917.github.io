Research

Research Areas & Projects

Research Interests

Low-Light Image Enhancement, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Hyperspectral Imaging, Image Processing, Convolutional Neural Networks, Real-World Vision Problems

Research Areas

Low-Light Image Enhancement: Developing deep learning-based methods to restore and enhance images captured under extremely low-light or degraded conditions, targeting improved visual quality and performance for downstream vision tasks.

Computer Vision: Investigating real-world vision challenges including image restoration, object recognition, and scene understanding using modern deep neural architectures.

Hyperspectral Imaging: Exploring spectral-spatial analysis and classification techniques for hyperspectral image data using deep learning.

Supervision

PhD research supervised by Dr. Abhisek Paul and Dr. (HAG) Dharmendra Singh at IIIT Vadodara.
