Professional Biography

Gaurav Singh is a PhD Scholar at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Vadodara, working under the supervision of Dr. Abhisek Paul and Dr. (HAG) Dharmendra Singh. His primary research focus is on image processing and deep learning, particularly low-light image enhancement and real-world computer vision challenges.

He received his B.Tech degree from SOA University, Odisha in 2021 with a CGPA of 8.67. Before joining IIIT Vadodara for his PhD (2024), he worked as a Data Scientist at ZS Associates and as a Software Engineer at Cognizant.

He has served as a Teaching Assistant for courses including Object-Oriented Programming (OOPS), Software Engineering, and C Programming at IIIT Vadodara. He is also serving as Teaching Assistant for the AICTE QIP program on Artificial Intelligence (June – December 2025).
