Professional Biography

Gaurav Singh is a PhD scholar at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Vadodara, working under the supervision of Dr. Abhisek Paul and Dr. (HAG) Dharmendra Singh. His research focuses on image processing and deep learning, with a primary emphasis on low-light image enhancement and real-world computer vision problems.

Prior to joining IIIT Vadodara for his PhD (2024), he worked as a Data Scientist at ZS Associates and as a Software Engineer at Cognizant. He completed his B.Tech from SOA University with an outstanding CGPA of 8.67.

He has served as a Teaching Assistant for courses including Object-Oriented Programming, Software Engineering, and C Programming at IIIT Vadodara. He is also a Teaching Assistant for the AICTE QIP program on Artificial Intelligence (June–December 2025).
